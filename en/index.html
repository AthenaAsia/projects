Recent updates

2026-09

CueFlow — Notation standardization completed a full loop: ask an outside professional, fold the answers into production, then re-verify in the same screen. Decisions we had been handling piece by piece — how rests are written, how tempo is shown — are now fixed as product-wide standards, and the reviewer re-runs the conversion in their own account to confirm the result. The review itself is now a fixed form too: the number of spots that must be fixed before the score is usable, the reviewer’s own estimated correction time to a deliverable state, and a verdict of usable / usable after fixes / not usable. We also extended the review to a piece with different instrumentation, with part-by-part checks continuing. For that piece the reviewer went through every page, and we corrected how notes are grouped, how rests are written and which way stems point according to the time signature. The basic theory rules now apply across every piece rather than one, and we are widening their coverage time signature by time signature. We also fixed the review screen showing nothing but a run of numbers on pieces with long stretches of rest: long rests are now shown as a single block, and a comment can be attached to the start of that range. We also built a verification module that credits stablecoin deposits only once they are confirmed, and only once (test network only; commercial use not yet decided).

2026-09

Concafe OS — Ran a design and code review focused on where the time-clock terminal meets the core system, surfacing 13 issues to fix before go-live, such as clocking in on consecutive days and recovering from network outages. Eleven were resolved through an automated AI fix loop; the rest are parked pending business decisions and expert advice.

2026-09

Copyright Management System — Built a "disclosure portal" into the existing system so rights holders can check contract-based revenue and documents at any time. It is designed so the operator cannot hide information, backed by a tamper-evident audit log. We also added a hiring aid that lets applicants from a sign-up form be auditioned and rated on the spot. That hiring aid now covers first-round interviews too: slots are pulled in automatically from the booking calendar, impressions and decisions are recorded, and the people involved are notified. Interview recordings are kept in private storage only authorized staff can open, with every playback and deletion logged. We also added inbound inquiry handling: messages addressed to the rights holder are pulled into the system, foreign-language ones get an automatic Japanese translation and a summary of the key points, a draft reply is prepared on the spot, and the reply can be sent straight from the system.
